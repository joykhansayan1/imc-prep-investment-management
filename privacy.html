Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile application IMC Prep (the “App”).

Summary

  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Study progress is stored on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit / App Store (iOS) or by Google via Google Play Billing (Android).

Information we process

On-device data: exam date, selected unit, practice answers, bookmarks, streaks, settings and similar study state. This data stays on your device unless you back up your device with Apple or Google or restore purchases.

Purchases: Subscription and lifetime purchases are handled by Apple (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We receive purchase entitlements through StoreKit or Google Play Billing; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Support email: If you contact us at dmjks120@gmail.com, we process the content of your message to respond.

Notifications: If you enable study reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. You can disable them in Settings or in iOS / Android system settings.

No tracking

The App does not track you across apps or websites for advertising. NSPrivacyTracking is false. The Android app uses no advertising ID.

Data retention

On-device study data remains until you delete the App or reset progress. Support emails are retained only as needed to assist you.

Children

The App is intended for adult professional exam candidates and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Disable reminders in the App or system settings.
  • Delete the App to remove on-device study data.
  • Manage subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings, or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions on Android.

International users

The App may be available worldwide via the App Store and Google Play. Processing occurs on your device and via Apple’s or Google’s systems.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
